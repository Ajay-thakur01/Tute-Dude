About The Earth

  • Earth is the densest planet in the Solar System and the largest of the four terrestrial planets.
  • Earth's gravity interacts with other objects in space, especially the Sun and the Moon, which is Earth's only natural satellite.
  • Earth orbits around the Sun in 365.26 days, a period known as an Earth year.
  • Earth's axis of rotation is tilted with respect to its orbital plane, producing seasons on Earth.
